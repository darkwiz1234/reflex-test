About Reflex Test

The Reflex Test game measures your reaction speed by tracking how quickly you respond to visual cues. Reaction time is a key indicator of how efficiently your brain and nervous system process information. On average, human reaction time to visual stimuli is around 250 milliseconds, though athletes and gamers often train to reduce this to under 200 ms. Faster reflexes can improve performance in sports, driving, and everyday tasks. Studies show that consistent practice with reaction games can enhance focus, hand-eye coordination, and cognitive processing speed. This game provides a fun way to challenge yourself, track your progress, and see how your reflexes compare over time.
